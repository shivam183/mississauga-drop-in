# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
By taking part, you agree to uphold it.

## In short

- Be welcoming, kind and patient, whatever someone's background or experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or publishing other people's private
  information.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainers privately through
GitHub (see the profile of a maintainer listed in the repository). Reports are
kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues, or ban
contributors, in line with the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
